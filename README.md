# join-our-team


At CopyAI, we believe AI will usher in a new era of human creativity, thinking, and 
innovation. Our generative AI platform is purpose-built to drive these outcomes for
our customers, giving them the ability to design and create high-impact, high-
leverage AI workflows that power their business. Here are [interesting videos](https://www.youtube.com/@Copyai/videos) 
showing Copy.ai through the lens of a marketer or salesperson.

On the engineering team, we're focused on developing magical
experiences for our customers. We have solid traction but are still a young 
company and we are looking for talented engineers who want to make a difference 
and want to be challenged with complex and interesting problems. 

We leverage the latest LLMs in our workflow platform and use an Airbyte K8 cluster for our data ingestion. 
We're committed to having a reliable, scalable, and flexible platform. All the while, doing 
our very best to make building as enjoyable as possible. We believe maintaining a great developer
experience is critical to deploying high-quality code to our users.

Our team is remotely distributed across the United States, Canada, and Brazil. We use 
Linear for projects, Roam for virtual meetings, Notion for docs, and Slack for all 
other communication. We're not fans of email or meetings. The vast majority of 
time is spent on heads-down work and problem-solving.

Right now, we are working mainly in TypeScript, Postgres, MongoDB and Redis, 
but we are more interested in good problem-solving skills vs. experience with any 
specific tools.

We are a social group, and we meet up for team-building 
activities. We meet up with the rest of the company regularly. Some of our trips in the past
have been to Cancun, Miami, and Park City, Utah. We spend our mornings getting everyone on the
same page and the afternoons and evenings playing.

Our goal is for team members to be able to look back and see their impact as we 
grow and evolve. There’s a lot to take on, but we trust they are up to the challenge. 

If this sounds like the kind of team you might want work with, please check out our open roles and let us know!
